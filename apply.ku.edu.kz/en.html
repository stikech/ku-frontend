Guaranteed dormitory placement upon admission

Every out-of-town applicant has the opportunity to stay in a dormitory at an affordable price!

The student campus houses 3 student dormitories and an academic building with comfortable living rooms. The dormitories are located in close proximity to the academic buildings and other university facilities, making it easy to access classes, the library, sports facilities, and other events.

Our dormitories are not just a place to live, but a comfortable and safe space where students can feel at home and focus on their academic and personal development.

International exchange programs

We are pleased to offer you the opportunity to immerse yourself in a unique international educational experience through academic mobility programs.

Academic mobility is a unique opportunity to broaden your horizons and receive education abroad or at other universities in Kazakhstan. We offer various academic mobility formats, including semester exchange programs, double degree programs, and much more.

Regardless of the path you choose, academic mobility will open doors to a world of knowledge and opportunities.

Internships, summer schools, conferences

Students and master's degree candidates can choose internships based on their interests and professional goals, and they often provide valuable experience that helps them develop their skills and expand their knowledge in a specific field.

Participating in conferences is a great opportunity to present your research, exchange experiences with colleagues from other universities, and receive feedback from experienced professionals in your field. Participation in such events can contribute to the professional growth of students and expand their professional network.

In summer schools, our students annually participate in intensive educational programs, usually organized over several weeks in the summer, offering students the opportunity to study a specific topic or field in depth.

Active student life

Student life at the university encompasses a wide array of opportunities for participation in athletic, creative, volunteer, and scientific activities, as well as in student governance.

All these facets of student life create a dynamic and vibrant environment at our university, where every student can develop, unlock their potential, and actively engage in campus life.

The Student Government, Youth Affairs Committee, and Student Union represent students’ interests, organize various events and initiatives, and serve as a conduit between the student body and the university administration.
