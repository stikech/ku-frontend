Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a refrigerator in the room?

Only some graduates leave their refrigerators in the rooms, but you can bring your own!

Are there cockroaches or bedbugs in the dormitory?

If cleanliness and order are maintained in the room, there are no insects!

Can I move into the same room with my sister/friend?

We always accommodate students’ preferences! You can move in with your acquaintances and clarify all the details during the move-in period.

How many people live in a room?

Our dormitories include rooms for 2 to 5 people, depending on the room size.

What is the difference between a corridor-type and a block-based dormitory?

In block-based dormitories, rooms are grouped into separate blocks, each with its own bathroom. In Hall-based dormitories, all rooms are located along a hallway on both sides.
